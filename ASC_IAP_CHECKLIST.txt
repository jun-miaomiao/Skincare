App Store Connect — IAP checklist (manual; Guideline 2.1b)
=========================================================
1. Business → Paid Apps Agreement must be Active.
2. For each product (monthly / yearly / lifetime):
   complete localization, pricing, subscription group,
   and App Review Screenshot (paywall selecting a plan).
3. Submit In-App Purchases for review with the new app binary.
4. Resolution Center: IAPs submitted; paywall reload CTA fixed;
   CosIng / TFDA (open data) citations added in-app.
